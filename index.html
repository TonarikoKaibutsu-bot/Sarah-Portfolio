Implementation philosophy

The framework

This is the backbone of the portfolio. Every project story should map back to it.

  1. 01 Discovery

    What problem are we solving, and what does success look like?

  2. 02 Stakeholder buy-in

    Who is impacted, who approves, and who owns the work after launch?

  3. 03 Requirements & MVP

    Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves so the first version is useful, not bloated.

  4. 04 Testing & validation

    Prove the process meets the intended need before calling it done.

  5. 05 Deployment & adoption

    Roll out training, support paths, and role clarity so users know what to do.

  6. 06 Continuous improvement

    Watch for friction, rework, and recurring questions; then improve the system.
