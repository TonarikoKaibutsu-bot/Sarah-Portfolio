# Case Study Draft: Redundant Safety Nets

## Background
A team continued using legacy checks after a new system introduced better monitoring, reporting, and traceability.

## Problem
Controls that once protected the process may no longer have been adding value. Some had become duplicate work layered on top of the new system.

## My Role
- Partnered with a counterpart at another facility to review current workflows.
- Identified which safety nets still protected quality and which could potentially be removed.
- Focused on reducing unnecessary work without weakening food safety or audit readiness.

## Evaluation Questions
- What risk was this safety net originally designed to control?
- Does the current system already control or monitor that risk?
- What evidence would justify removing or changing the step?
- Who needs to approve the change?
- How will we verify the new workflow remains effective?

## Future State
A leaner workflow where associates perform controls that actually add value, while redundant work is removed through a documented and approved change process.
